# Experiment timeline

Attempts and outcomes recoverable from the saved reports, archived studies, configs, and Git history, through **November 12, 2026**. Repeated parameter settings are grouped into their experiment rather than listed as separate attempts.

Dates use **America/Los_Angeles**. Dated evaluations follow their saved report-update timestamps. **“By” and “approximately” mark work without an exact saved experiment date**; order within those groups follows the study dependencies where recoverable. File-copy timestamps are not treated as experiment dates. Planned work without a recorded run is excluded.

**Metric key:** Hit@10 measures whether the top ten retrieved messages contain an annotated reference. Accuracy measures Qwen's final answer. These are different outcomes. Early studies use different memory units and question cohorts. Historical V2 validation reports average six non-forget types; after the November 12 exclusion, five remain. Their percentages should not be compared directly without accounting for those changes.
